Seasons' Eatings

What's actually in season near you - and when to sow it, grow it, and eat it.

Seasons' Eatings is a UK app that tracks what's in season for eating right now, reminds you when to plant and harvest the fruit and veg you're growing based on your own postcode's weather, and gives you seasonal recipes built around what's actually good this month.

Sow and harvest reminders

Personalised to your postcode's weather, with spacing, companion-planting and frost-timing guidance per crop.

Frost alerts

A heads-up as a cold snap approaches for anything you've got in the ground, escalating as it gets closer.

Home-screen widget

What's in season, or your nearest upcoming task, right on your home screen.

Seasonal recipes

Recipes built around what's actually in season, not what's flown in from the other side of the world.
