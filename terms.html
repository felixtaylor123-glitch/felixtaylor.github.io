Growing advice and predictions are estimates, not guarantees

The app's sow/harvest timing, frost alerts, watering advice, and yield predictions are generated from general horticultural sources (including the RHS, Gardeners' World, and Garden Organic) and third-party weather data, combined with your specific postcode's forecast. They are estimates intended to help your decision-making, not guarantees. Real growing outcomes depend on many factors outside the app's knowledge - soil condition, pests, exact microclimate, how the plant is actually cared for, and weather forecasts themselves being imperfect. We're not liable for any loss of crops, produce, or gardening effort arising from following the app's guidance.

Frost alerts in particular rely on forecasts available at the time each alert is scheduled. Because the app has no backend to push updated alerts if a forecast changes after scheduling, a forecast that shifts between when an alert is set and when it fires may mean the alert no longer perfectly reflects real conditions - reopening the app periodically keeps alerts current. Always use your own judgement alongside the app, especially for anything you're relying on economically.
